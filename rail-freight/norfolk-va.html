Rail Freight Shipping in Norfolk, VA

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Norfolk is Norfolk Southern's namesake city and a critical coal export terminal. Lamberts Point is the largest coal transloading facility in the Northern Hemisphere, loading coal onto vessels for international export. The Port of Virginia's marine terminals also feature excellent rail connectivity for import and export traffic.

Railroads Serving Norfolk

Norfolk is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Norfolk metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Multimodal & Port Connections

Port of Virginia (Norfolk International Terminals, Virginia International Gateway) — major East Coast deep-water port with double-stack rail access.

Port access gives Norfolk shippers the advantage of rail-to-vessel transloading for international trade. Whether you are exporting or importing bulk commodities, the combination of rail and port infrastructure creates efficient multimodal supply chain options.
