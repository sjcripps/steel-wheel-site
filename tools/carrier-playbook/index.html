Become Payer of Freight — readiness checklist

Until these are done, run deals as shipper-pays + LOA — the shipper carries credit, you run the move. Per-road specifics live in each card’s “Credit & Payer Setup” section below.

Ready-today prep

  • Entity docs packet — articles of incorporation / MS registration document, W-9, EIN (CPKC requires the actual incorporation document; legal name goes on bills of lading as the Bill To Party)
  • DUNS number — asked by 4 of 6 roads (BNSF, CSX, NS, CPKC); free at dnb.com
  • Officer / contact info sheet — principal officer name + title (NS requires an officer signature before review starts), AP contact, and the person responsible for paying freight bills (NS/CPKC ask separately)
  • 3 trade references w/ contacts — transportation-related preferred (NS wants unaffiliated transportation companies; needed by CSX, NS, CPKC)
  • Commodity / volume / credit-amount estimates per road — weekly credit (CSX), monthly credit + cars AND trailers per month (NS), annual shipment value USD (CPKC), credit amount requested (BNSF)
  • Establish a sales-rep contact per road — every application asks who your rep is; the sales funnel comes FIRST (BNSF makes rep contact a formal prerequisite)
  • LOA / authorization-letter template ready for client signatures (UP Letter of Authority; BNSF client authorization letter — signed by the client or sent from the client’s email domain)

Needs business bank account

  • Open SWL business checking
  • Bank reference sheet — bank name, address, contact/officer name, phone, email, account # (CSX, NS, CPKC, KCSM all require it)
  • CSX ACH-Debit setup — the instant-accept lever (needs routing/ABA + account number on the form)
  • NS / KCSM account-number fields — NS wants checking-vs-savings + types of loans; KCSM wants the bank-account list

No road asks for a personal guarantee — credit is entity-based; weak financials route to the prepay/security path instead. Checkboxes autosave in this browser (localStorage).
