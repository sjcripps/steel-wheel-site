Rail logistics services in Nashville

A Nashville shipper moving automotive, coal and building materials by rail can route on CSX or Norfolk Southern, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between CSX and NS and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, coal, building materials and consumer goods that means sourcing autoracks, open-top hoppers and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Nashville (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Nashville lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Nashville

Nashville is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Nashville metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Common questions about rail freight in Nashville

Which railroads serve Nashville?

Nashville, TN is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, with major facilities including Radnor Yard and Kayne Avenue Yard. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle rail freight in Nashville?

Yes. Steel Wheel Logistics is a national rail logistics company based in Petal, Mississippi, and works Nashville lanes remotely as an outsourced rail department: routing, pricing, railroad coordination, transload, demurrage management and railcar sourcing. There is no walk-in location; the work is done by phone, email and the railroads' own systems.
