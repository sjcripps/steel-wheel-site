Rail Freight Shipping in Baton Rouge, LA

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Baton Rouge anchors a massive petrochemical corridor along the Mississippi River between New Orleans and the capital. Union Pacific and Canadian National serve the region's refineries and chemical plants, moving petroleum products, plastics, and chemicals by rail. The port handles significant grain and fertilizer volumes as well.

Railroads Serving Baton Rouge

Baton Rouge is served by Union Pacific and Canadian National, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Baton Rouge metro area.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including intermodal, industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

Canadian National

Canadian National Railway operates a transcontinental network spanning Canada and the central U.S. from the Gulf Coast to the Great Lakes. CN handles intermodal, petroleum, forest products, and grain.
