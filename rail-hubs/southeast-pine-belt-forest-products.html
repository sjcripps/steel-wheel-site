Southeast Pine Belt (Forest Products)

Origin region · Southeast Pine Belt, MS · Class I service: CSX, NS, CN

MS, AL, GA, SC pine belt produces wood pulp, cellulose rolls, kraft liner board, and lumber. CN (former Illinois Central) and CSX/NS move product to Gulf and Atlantic break bulk terminals.

Commodities through this hub

  • Lumber & Wood Products by rail — Centerbeam flat, 75–95 tons/car
  • Paper & Pulp by rail — High-cube boxcar, 70–90 tons/car
  • Wood Pellets by rail — Covered hopper, 90–100 tons/car
