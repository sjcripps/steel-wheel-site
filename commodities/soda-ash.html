Soda Ash Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Natural sodium carbonate from the world's largest trona deposit at Green River, Wyoming — a one-origin commodity that rails everywhere glass is made.

Soda ash logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates soda ash carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Soda ash weighs out at 100–112 tons per car (roughly 58–65 lb per cubic foot) well before the car is full, so we size covered hoppers to the weight limit and the receiving track's capacity rather than to cube. Typical end uses are glassmaking (the largest use), detergents, chemicals, lithium processing — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a bulk transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source covered hoppers on lease or per trip.

Common questions

Can soda ash be shipped by rail?

Yes. Soda Ash moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Unit and manifest trains from Green River to domestic glass plants and to West Coast ports — one of the largest US rail export commodities by tonnage..

How many tons of soda ash fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 100–112 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does soda ash move in?

Covered hopper (~3,500 cu ft). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of soda ash?

Roughly 58–65 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.

Yes — we price and coordinate soda ash carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in covered hoppers at 100–112 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
