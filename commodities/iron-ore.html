Iron Ore by Rail

Taconite pellets from Minnesota's Iron Range — so dense that purpose-built ore cars are barely half the length of a grain hopper and still hit the axle limit.

RailcarOre cars (short) or open-top hopper
Typical net load95–110 tons per car
Bulk density~130–150 lb/cu ft
Loading constraintWeighs out (weight-limited)
STCC10112 — look up related codes in the STCC Finder

What it's used for

Blast-furnace and DRI steelmaking.

How it moves

Captive unit trains from the Mesabi Range to Great Lakes ore docks, then lakers to lower-lakes steel mills; some all-rail moves to mills.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Minnesota: 53.6 million tons of metallic ores moved by rail to, from and within the state in 2019 (Minnesota State Rail Plan 2025, p. 34).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Wisconsin: 11.3 million tons of iron ores terminated by rail in the state in 2019 (Wisconsin State Rail Plan 2026, p. 139).
  • Ohio: 5.9 million tons of metallic ores originated by rail in the state in 2022 (Ohio State Rail Plan 2025, p. 77).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can iron ore be shipped by rail?

Yes. Iron Ore moves regularly on the North American rail network in ore carss. Captive unit trains from the Mesabi Range to Great Lakes ore docks, then lakers to lower-lakes steel mills; some all-rail moves to mills..

How many tons of iron ore fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 95–110 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does iron ore move in?

Ore cars (short) or open-top hopper. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of iron ore?

Roughly 130–150 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.
