Rail Freight Shipping in Knoxville, TN

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Knoxville is an east Tennessee rail hub served by Norfolk Southern and CSX. The city sits at the junction of rail corridors passing through the Appalachian coal fields, handling significant coal traffic from eastern Kentucky and Virginia mines. NS's mainline through Knoxville connects the Southeast to the Ohio Valley.

Railroads Serving Knoxville

Knoxville is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Knoxville metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.
