Rail Freight Shipping in Chattanooga, TN

2 Class I Railroads · 3 Major Terminals

Chattanooga is a critical rail junction in southeast Tennessee where Norfolk Southern operates one of its key intermodal terminals. The city sits at the intersection of NS corridors connecting Atlanta, Knoxville, and Nashville, making it a strategic interchange point for intermodal freight. The Volkswagen assembly plant adds automotive rail volumes.

Railroads Serving Chattanooga

Chattanooga is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Chattanooga metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for intermodal, coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles intermodal, coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around Chattanooga include:

  • DeButts Yard (NS)
  • Wauhatchie Yard (NS)
