Soybean Meal by Rail

The protein half of the soybean crush, moving from Midwest crush plants to feed mills and export terminals.

RailcarCovered hopper (5,200+ cu ft)
Typical net load95–105 tons per car
Bulk density~38–42 lb/cu ft

STCC20923 — look up related codes in the STCC Finder

What it's used for

Poultry, swine, dairy and aquaculture feed — the backbone protein of animal rations.

How it moves

Single cars and blocks to feed mills; unit trains to export. New crush capacity built for renewable diesel has added meal tonnage across the network.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Washington: 5.9 million tons of animal feed moved by rail to, from, within and through the state in 2016 (Washington State Rail Plan 2019, p. 144).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Missouri: 2.4 million tons of soybean oil and by-products moved by rail outbound from the state in 2018 (Missouri State Rail Plan 2022, p. 51).
  • South Dakota: 401,000 tons of soybean cake, meal and screenings moved by rail outbound from the state in 2019 (South Dakota State Rail Plan 2022, p. 111).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can soybean meal be shipped by rail?

Yes. Soybean Meal moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Single cars and blocks to feed mills; unit trains to export.

How many tons of soybean meal fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 95–105 net tons per car.

What railcar does soybean meal move in?

Covered hopper (5,200+ cu ft). Car choice varies with grade and lane.

What is the bulk density of soybean meal?

Roughly 38–42 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.
