Port of New Orleans, LA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are mineral fuels (about $3,425 million), fertilizer (about $1,854 million), iron and steel (about $1,838 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by BNSF Railway, Union Pacific, CN, CPKC, Norfolk Southern and CSX, which is what makes the New Orleans gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. Six Class Is via NOPB; river bulk terminals up to Baton Rouge. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
