Propane Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Liquefied petroleum gas rides in thick-shelled pressure cars from fractionators to regional storage ahead of heating season.

Propane logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates propane carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Propane is carried by volume rather than weight, about 60–70 tons per car in pressure tank cars, so we spec the car class and fittings first and price the lane per car. Typical end uses are home heating, crop drying, petrochemical feedstock — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a tank transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source pressure tank cars on lease or per trip, and on propane we line up car supply ahead of the seasonal peak.

A typical load is 60–70 net tons per car.

What railcar does propane / lpg move in?

Pressure tank car (DOT-112, ~33,500 gal). Car choice varies with grade and lane.
