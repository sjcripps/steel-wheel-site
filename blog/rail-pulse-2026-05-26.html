Rail Pulse — Week Ending May 16, 2026

May 26, 2026 · 3 min read · Rail Pulse

Rail Pulse is Steel Wheel Logistics' weekly read on what actually moved on the U.S. rail network last week. Every figure is sourced from public AAR, STB, EIA, and USDA filings and linked at the bottom. Every word of analysis is anchored to those numbers — no estimates, no extrapolation.

The Number

Carload traffic for the week ending May 16, 2026 totaled 230497 units, up 0.6 percent year-over-year, while intermodal surged 7.3 percent to 280719 units. Combined rail traffic reached 511216 cars and containers, a 4.2 percent gain.

Network Health

Color codes: green = speed ≥24 mph or dwell <22 hrs; yellow = speed 21–24 mph or dwell 22–26 hrs; red = speed <21 mph or dwell >26 hrs. Source: STB EP724 weekly Class I performance filings.

Fuel Watch

Diesel fuel (ULSD) settled at 5.596 per gallon for the week ending May 18, down 0.76 percent from 5.639 the prior week. Month-to-date average stands at 5.625, a 2.25 percent rise versus April's 5.5012. Class I carriers' fuel surcharges remain stable into June: BNSF 61 cents/mile, UP 69 cents/mile, NS 76 cents/mile, CSXT 44 cents/mile, CN 77 cents/mile, and CPKC 74 cents/mile.

Commodity Spotlight

Grain carloads jumped 9.8 percent year-over-year for the week ending May 16, with 23173 units moved. Petroleum and petroleum products also posted a 9.8 percent gain at 11278 carloads, while forest products climbed 8.9 percent to 8636 units. Coal declined 6.2 percent to 55302 carloads, dragging overall carload growth.
