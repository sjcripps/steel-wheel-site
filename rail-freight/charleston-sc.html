Rail Logistics Services in Charleston, SC

2 Class I Railroads · 1 Major Terminal ·Port Access

Charleston is a growing Southeast port city where CSX and Norfolk Southern connect international import and export trade to inland markets. The South Carolina Ports Authority has invested heavily in rail infrastructure, including the Inland Port at Dillon, which extends the port's reach into the Carolinas and Virginia via Norfolk Southern rail service.

Rail logistics services in Charleston

A Charleston shipper moving automotive, paper products and consumer goods by rail can route on CSX or Norfolk Southern, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between CSX and NS and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, paper products, consumer goods and machinery that means sourcing autoracks and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Charleston's port access adds a rail-to-vessel option for export and import cargo; we arrange the transload leg and the terminal handoff so the rail move and the vessel schedule line up. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Charleston lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.

Railroads Serving Charleston

Charleston is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Charleston metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Charleston, SC is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, with major facilities including Palmetto Railways. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.
