Port of Norfolk-Newport News, VA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are plastics and resin (about $1,731 million), organic chemicals (about $1,316 million), steel articles and pipe (about $1,147 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by Norfolk Southern and CSX, which is what makes the Norfolk-Newport News gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
