Rail Logistics Services in Baltimore, MD

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Baltimore is a key East Coast port city served by CSX and Norfolk Southern. The Port of Baltimore is the nation's top auto import port and a significant coal export terminal. CSX's Howard Street Tunnel is being expanded to accommodate double-stack trains, which will dramatically increase capacity through the corridor.

Rail logistics services in Baltimore

A Baltimore shipper moving coal, automotive and grain by rail can route on CSX or Norfolk Southern, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between CSX and NS and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For coal, automotive, grain and steel that means sourcing open-top hoppers, autoracks and covered hoppers, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Baltimore's port access adds a rail-to-vessel option for export and import cargo; we arrange the transload leg and the terminal handoff so the rail move and the vessel schedule line up. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Baltimore lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.

Railroads Serving Baltimore

Baltimore is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Baltimore metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Common questions about rail freight in Baltimore

Which railroads serve Baltimore?

Baltimore, MD is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, with major facilities including Bayview Yard and Curtis Bay Coal Terminal. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle rail freight in Baltimore?

Yes. Steel Wheel Logistics is a national rail logistics company based in Petal, Mississippi, and works Baltimore lanes remotely as an outsourced rail department: routing, pricing, railroad coordination, transload, demurrage management and railcar sourcing. There is no walk-in location; the work is done by phone, email and the railroads' own systems.
