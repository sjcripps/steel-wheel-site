The bottom-of-the-barrel refinery byproduct — fuel-grade petcoke moves like coal, anode-grade feeds aluminum smelters.

What it's used for

Cement kilns and power generation (fuel grade), aluminum anodes (calcined grade), export.

How it moves

Gulf Coast refineries to cement plants, export docks and calciners; handled with coal-style rotary dump equipment.

Where it flows

  • Toledo (Lake Erie Coal & Iron Ore Port) — Toledo, OH (CSX, NS)

Common questions

Can petroleum coke be shipped by rail?

Yes. Petroleum Coke moves regularly on the North American rail network in open-top hopper or rotary gondolas. Gulf Coast refineries to cement plants, export docks and calciners; handled with coal-style rotary dump equipment..

How many tons of petroleum coke fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 100–115 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does petroleum coke move in?

Open-top hopper or rotary gondola. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of petroleum coke?

Roughly 45–55 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.
