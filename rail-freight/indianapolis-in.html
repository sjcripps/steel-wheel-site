Rail Freight Shipping in Indianapolis, IN

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Indianapolis is a crossroads for CSX and Norfolk Southern freight in the heart of the Midwest. The city's central location makes it a natural hub for automotive parts, grain, and intermodal freight. CSX's Avon Yard handles classification of railcars moving between eastern and midwestern markets.

Railroads Serving Indianapolis

Indianapolis is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Indianapolis metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles intermodal, coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for intermodal, coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.
