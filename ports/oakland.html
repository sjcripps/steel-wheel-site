Port of Oakland, CA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are coffee and tea (about $608 million), plastics and resin (about $527 million), rubber (about $496 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by BNSF Railway and Union Pacific, which is what makes the Oakland gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
