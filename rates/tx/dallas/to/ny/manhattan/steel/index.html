About this lane

The Dallas, TX-Manhattan, NY rail corridor primarily serves the transportation of steel coils, plates, and bars, with Amtrak (AMTK) as the main carrier. This corridor plays a critical role in facilitating the movement of heavy industrial goods between major markets, supporting both regional and national supply chains. Notably, interchanges with freight railroads enhance routing efficiency and connectivity, allowing for streamlined logistics across the eastern United States.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to ship steel coils / plate / bar by rail from Dallas to Manhattan?

Rail transit on this corridor typically falls in the 7-12 day range door-to-door, depending on car supply, interchange dwell, and any required transload. Steel Wheel Logistics does not publish guaranteed transits because every actual move depends on current network conditions and your specific origin and destination facilities.

What does the fuel surcharge add on a Dallas to Manhattan rail move?

Each Class I publishes a mileage-based fuel surcharge (FSC) tied to the EIA highway-diesel fuel index. AMTK and any participating carriers each apply their own published cents-per-mile factor to the miles they handle. FSC totals on this lane re-derive monthly as fuel prices move; the figure shown is current as of the date stamped in the hero card above.
