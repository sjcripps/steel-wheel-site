Tulsa (BNSF / UP Crude & Grain)

Major rail hub / inland gateway · Tulsa, OK · Class I service: BNSF, UP

Cushing crude oil hub sits 60 miles southwest — pipeline-rail interface for the WTI benchmark. BNSF Cherokee Yard and UP yards serve refining and petrochemical-adjacent traffic. Wheat outbound from Oklahoma elevators.
