Gulf Coast Chemical Corridor

Origin region · Gulf Coast Chemical Corridor, TX · Class I service:

Largest petrochemical manufacturing corridor in the world — Houston Ship Channel to Baton Rouge. Tank cars and covered hoppers distribute chemicals and plastics to every corner of North America.

Commodities through this hub

  • Dry Fertilizer (Urea, DAP, MAP) by rail — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • Potash by rail — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Cement by rail — Pressure-differential covered hopper, 100–115 tons/car
  • Frac Sand by rail — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Plastic Pellets (Resin) by rail — Jumbo covered hopper, 85–95 tons/car
  • Industrial Chemicals (Liquid) by rail — General-service or specialized tank car, 80–100 tons/car
