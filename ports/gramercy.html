Port of Gramercy, LA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are fertilizer (about $397 million), iron and steel (about $317 million), ores and slag (about $311 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CN, CPKC and Union Pacific, which is what makes the Gramercy gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. Lower Mississippi bulk terminals: Gramercy, Convent, Reserve, Burnside. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
