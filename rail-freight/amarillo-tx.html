Rail Freight Shipping in Amarillo, TX

1 Class I Railroad · 2 Major Terminals

Amarillo sits on BNSF's Southern Transcon — one of the busiest freight rail corridors in the world connecting Los Angeles to Chicago. The Texas Panhandle city handles grain, cattle feed, and petroleum products from the surrounding agricultural and energy regions. BNSF's high-speed intermodal trains pass through Amarillo daily on the Transcon route.

Railroads Serving Amarillo

Amarillo is served by BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Amarillo metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for intermodal, coal, grain, and industrial products.
