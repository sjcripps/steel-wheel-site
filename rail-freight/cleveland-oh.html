Rail Freight Shipping in Cleveland, OH

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Cleveland is a legacy steel and heavy industry rail hub on the southern shore of Lake Erie. CSX and Norfolk Southern handle significant volumes of steel, iron ore, and coal through the metro area. The Port of Cleveland provides lake vessel-to-rail interchange for bulk commodities arriving via the Great Lakes.

Railroads Serving Cleveland

Cleveland is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Cleveland metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles intermodal, coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for intermodal, coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around Cleveland include:

  • Rockport Yard (NS)

Intermodal & Port Connections

Port of Cleveland on Lake Erie — handles iron ore and bulk materials via Great Lakes shipping.

Port access gives Cleveland shippers the advantage of rail-to-vessel transloading for international trade. Whether you are exporting bulk commodities or importing containerized freight, the combination of rail and port infrastructure creates efficient multimodal supply chain options.
