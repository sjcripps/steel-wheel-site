Soybeans Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

The premier export grain. Soybeans move in dense harvest surges from the Midwest to Gulf and Pacific Northwest export elevators and to domestic crush plants.

Soybeans logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates soybeans carload moves nationwide — single cars, multi-car blocks and unit-train sets — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Soybeans weigh out at 100–110 tons per car (roughly 46–48 lb per cubic foot) well before the car is full, so we size covered hoppers to the weight limit and the receiving track's capacity rather than to cube. Typical end uses are crushing into soybean meal and oil, direct export, food products — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a bulk transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: on shuttle and unit-train cycles the clock runs on the whole set, so loading and release windows are agreed before the train is ordered. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source covered hoppers on lease or per trip.

Common questions

Can soybeans be shipped by rail?

Yes. Soybeans moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Heavily unit-train and shuttle; the PNW export lane is one of the busiest grain corridors in the world Oct–Feb..

How many tons of soybeans fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 100–110 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does soybeans move in?

Covered hopper (4,750–5,200 cu ft). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of soybeans?

Roughly 46–48 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle soybeans rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate soybeans carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in covered hoppers at 100–110 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
