Rail Pulse — Week Ending May 2, 2026

May 12, 2026 · 3 min read · Rail Pulse

Rail Pulse is Steel Wheel Logistics' weekly read on what actually moved on the U.S. rail network last week. Every figure is sourced from public AAR, STB, EIA, and USDA filings and linked at the bottom. Every word of analysis is anchored to those numbers — no estimates, no extrapolation.

The Number

518,773 total rail units moved in the week ending May 2, 2026, up 3.9% year over year and 1.4% from the prior week, as carloads and intermodal both posted gains.

Network Health

As of May 6, 2026, BNSF runs green on speed at 24.9 mph but yellow on dwell at 22.3 hours. UP is green on both, posting 24.9 mph and 20.0 hours dwell. CSX is green on speed at 24.48155458601284 mph and green on dwell at 20.74 hours. NS is red on speed at 20.1 mph and yellow on dwell at 23.5 hours. CPKC is yellow on speed at 22.4 mph and green on dwell at 21.13 hours. CN speed data is unavailable this period; its dwell reads green at 18.52 hours.

Color codes: green = speed ≥24 mph or dwell <22 hrs; yellow = speed 21–24 mph or dwell 22–26 hrs; red = speed <21 mph or dwell >26 hrs. Source: STB EP724 weekly Class I performance filings.

Fuel Watch

Highway diesel came in at $5.639 per gallon as of May 11, 2026, essentially flat against the prior week's $5.64 and up 2.51% from last month's average of $5.5012. Current mileage-based fuel surcharge factors effective June 1 stand at 61 cents per mile for BNSF, 69 for UP, 76 for NS, 44 for CSXT, 77 for CN, and 74 for CPKC.

Commodity Spotlight

Metallic Ores and Metals posted the largest year-over-year move of any AAR commodity in the week ending May 2, 2026, surging 20.6% to 23,164 carloads. That compares to 19,357 carloads on a year-to-date weekly average basis, underscoring that this week's volume is running well ahead of the sustained pace. Shippers moving metals should note the week-over-week gain of 1,941 carloads, a 9.15% jump from the prior week's 21,223.
