Port of Corpus Christi, TX rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are mineral fuels (about $2,378 million), ores and slag (about $166 million), cement, salt, stone, sulfur (about $68 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by Union Pacific, BNSF Railway and CPKC, which is what makes the Corpus Christi gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
