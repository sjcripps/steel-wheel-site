Rail-Served Warehouses in Illinois

43 third-party warehouses in Illinois that take other companies' freight and sit on rail — 6 with rail service confirmed in the operator's own words, 6 with indoor storage confirmed. These are public and contract warehouses — bulk commodity storage, 3PL and distribution space you can rent — not private plants or retailer distribution centres that only handle their own goods.

Every facility here has been geocoded to its street address and measured against the federal rail network. A warehouse with rail lets you take a railcar to the building instead of trucking from a terminal, which is usually where the savings are on bulk moves.

How “rail-served” is determined here. Each facility's street address is geocoded and measured against the federal North American Rail Network — the same network our rate estimator routes on. Only facilities within a quarter mile of track are listed, and they are shown in two groups. Operator-confirmed means the operator's own website states rail service (a siding, a spur, a serving railroad, railcar spots) and we quote it verbatim. Near rail — unverified means the building sits close to track but the operator does not say it is served; a mainline passing a facility is not the same thing as a siding into it, so treat proximity alone as a lead, not a fact. This list is not exhaustive. A rail-served warehouse missing from it has not been ruled out — it means we have not yet confirmed the siding. Our rail map has known gaps (it lacks some industrial trackage, and does not cover Alaska or Mexico), and a facility can also be missing because we have no verified street address for it. If you know of a rail-served warehouse that is not here, tell us and we will check it. Listings are compiled from public sources; Steel Wheel Logistics does not own or operate these facilities and a listing does not imply a commercial relationship. Confirm siding condition, car capacity and current availability with the operator — or ask us and we will confirm it for you.
