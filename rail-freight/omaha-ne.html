Rail Logistics Services in Omaha, NE

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Omaha is the headquarters of Union Pacific Railroad and a major gateway for agricultural commodities moving from the Great Plains. The nearby Bailey Yard in North Platte is the world's largest classification yard. Omaha's rail infrastructure supports heavy grain, ethanol, and fertilizer traffic serving the agricultural heartland.

Rail logistics services in Omaha

A Omaha shipper moving grain, livestock feed and ethanol by rail can route on Union Pacific or BNSF, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between UP and BNSF and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For grain, livestock feed, ethanol and fertilizer that means sourcing covered hoppers, tank cars and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Omaha (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Omaha lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Omaha

Omaha is served by Union Pacific and BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Omaha metro area.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.
