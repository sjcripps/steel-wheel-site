Rail logistics services in Montgomery

A Montgomery shipper moving automotive, lumber and paper products by rail can route on CSX or Norfolk Southern, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between CSX and NS and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, lumber, paper products and consumer goods that means sourcing autoracks, centerbeam flatcars and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Montgomery (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Montgomery lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Montgomery

Montgomery is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Montgomery metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.
