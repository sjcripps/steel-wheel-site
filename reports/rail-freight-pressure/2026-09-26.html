5. Grain and railcars

Shuttle cars in the secondary market bid $525 a car over tariff for September 17 GTR, September delivery; non-shuttle $113 over. Class I grain tariffs step up in October: BNSF +$200 per car on most PNW origins, UP +$225, CPKC +$225, CN +$275 in September and +$330 from October to December 1 (USDA GTR).

Railcar leasing: GATX's Q2 2026 index shows renewals repricing +16.8% above expiring rates on 54-month terms at 98.0% utilization. Q3 results expected late October.

Sources: USDA Grain Transportation Report · GATX Q2 2026 results

7. Regulatory calendar

UP-NS merger, STB docket FD 36873. Summary-denial motions from BNSF, CSX and a shipper coalition were denied September 18. Notices of intent to participate are due September 30. Comments on the merits are due November 18, replies February 16, 2027. If your traffic touches NS and reaches UP or BNSF through New Orleans, Memphis or Meridian, the comment window is where gateway protections get asked for. Source

Reciprocal switching. The STB's January proposal to repeal the 2024 reciprocal switching rules (Part 1144) has closed comments; no final decision as of this issue. The 2024 inadequate-service rule remains on the books. Source

Section 232 wood products. Hardwood timber and lumber review due to the President October 1. Upholstered furniture to 30% and cabinets and vanities to 50% on January 1, 2027. Softwood lumber stays at 10%. Source
