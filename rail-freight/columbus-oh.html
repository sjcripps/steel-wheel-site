Rail Logistics Services in Columbus, OH

2 Class I Railroads · 1 Major Terminal

Columbus is a major freight hub in the Midwest, anchored by Norfolk Southern's Rickenbacker terminal — one of the busiest inland rail facilities in the eastern U.S. The city's central Ohio location makes it a natural distribution point for consumer goods serving the eastern half of the country.

Rail logistics services in Columbus

A Columbus shipper moving automotive, consumer goods and steel by rail can route on Norfolk Southern or CSX, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between NS and CSX and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, consumer goods, steel and building materials that means sourcing autoracks, boxcars and gondolas and coil cars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Columbus (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Columbus lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.

Railroads Serving Columbus

Columbus is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Columbus metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Common questions about rail freight in Columbus

Which railroads serve Columbus?

Columbus, OH is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, with major facilities including Buckeye Yard. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Yes.
