Corn by Rail

The highest-volume grain on North American rails. Feed corn moves from the Corn Belt to feedlots, ethanol plants, and Gulf/PNW export elevators.

RailcarCovered hopper (4,750–5,200 cu ft)
Typical net load100–110 tons per car
Bulk density~44–46 lb/cu ft
Loading constraintWeighs out (weight-limited)
STCC01132 — look up related codes in the STCC Finder

What it's used for

Animal feed, ethanol production, corn syrup and starch milling, export.

How it moves

Shuttle and unit trains of 75–110 cars dominate the export and ethanol lanes; single cars and 5–25 car blocks serve feed mills off short lines. Peak movement follows harvest (Oct–Dec).

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Texas: 48.8 million tons of farm products moved by rail in all directions in 2022 (Texas State Rail Plan 2024, p. 169).
  • Illinois: 46.5 million tons of farm products handled by the state's rail network in all directions (including through traffic) in 2019 (Illinois State Rail Plan 2023, p. 267).
  • Iowa: 34.3 million tons of farm products moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2022 (Iowa State Rail Plan 2025, p. 246).
  • Oklahoma: 30.7 million tons of farm products moved by rail in all directions, including traffic passing through the state in 2019 (Oklahoma State Rail Plan 2021, p. 97).
  • Idaho: 26.9 million tons of cereal grains moved by rail into, out of, within or through the state (through traffic included) in 2010 (Idaho State Rail Plan 2013, p. 65).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Missouri: 20.8 million tons of grain moved through the state by rail in 2018 (Missouri State Rail Plan 2022, p. 52).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can corn be shipped by rail?

Yes. Corn moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Shuttle and unit trains of 75–110 cars dominate the export and ethanol lanes; single cars and 5–25 car blocks serve feed mills off short lines.

How many tons of corn fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 100–110 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does corn move in?

Covered hopper (4,750–5,200 cu ft). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of corn?

Roughly 44–46 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.
