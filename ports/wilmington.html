Port of Wilmington, NC rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel import here, year to date through July 2026, is fertilizer (about $202 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CSX, which is what makes the Wilmington gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland.
