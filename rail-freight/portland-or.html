2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Portland is the Pacific Northwest's primary rail hub, served by BNSF and Union Pacific. The Port of Portland's grain elevators export wheat and other grains from the Columbia Basin and Great Plains. The city also handles lumber and forest products rail traffic from the surrounding timber region.

Rail logistics services in Portland

A Portland shipper moving grain, lumber and potash by rail can route on BNSF or Union Pacific, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between BNSF and UP and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For grain, lumber, potash and soda ash that means sourcing covered hoppers, centerbeam flatcars and autoracks, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Portland's port access adds a rail-to-vessel option for export and import cargo; we arrange the transload leg and the terminal handoff so the rail move and the vessel schedule line up. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Portland lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.

Railroads Serving Portland

Portland is served by BNSF and Union Pacific, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Portland metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

Common questions about rail freight in Portland

Which railroads serve Portland?

Portland, OR is served by BNSF and Union Pacific, with major facilities including Lake Yard and Willbridge Yard. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle rail freight in Portland?

Yes. Steel Wheel Logistics is a national rail logistics company based in Petal, Mississippi, and works Portland lanes remotely as an outsourced rail department: routing, pricing, railroad coordination, transload, demurrage management and railcar sourcing. There is no walk-in location; the work is done by phone, email and the railroads' own systems.
