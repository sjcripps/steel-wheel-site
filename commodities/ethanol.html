Ethanol by Rail

Fuel ethanol cannot move in product pipelines (it absorbs water), so nearly all of it rails from Corn Belt plants to blending terminals.

RailcarDOT-117 tank car (~28,500–30,000 gal)
Typical net load85–95 tons per car
Loading constraintWeighs out (weight-limited)
STCC28184 — look up related codes in the STCC Finder

What it's used for

Gasoline blending (E10/E15/E85), industrial alcohol.

How it moves

Unit trains from Iowa/Nebraska/Illinois plants to coastal and metro blending racks — one of the steadiest tank-car franchises on the network.

Common questions

Can ethanol be shipped by rail?

Yes. Ethanol moves regularly on the North American rail network in dot-117 tank cars. Unit trains from Iowa/Nebraska/Illinois plants to coastal and metro blending racks — one of the steadiest tank-car franchises on the network..

How many tons of ethanol fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 85–95 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does ethanol move in?

DOT-117 tank car (~28,500–30,000 gal). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.
