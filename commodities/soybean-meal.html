Soybean Meal Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

The protein half of the soybean crush, moving from Midwest crush plants to feed mills and export terminals.

Soybean meal logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates soybean meal carload moves nationwide — single cars, multi-car blocks and unit-train sets — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. At 38–42 lb per cubic foot soybean meal loads close to both limits at once (95–105 tons per car), so we match covered hoppers to the receiver's unloading method and check weight and cube on every load plan. Typical end uses are poultry, swine, dairy and aquaculture feed — the backbone protein of animal rations — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a bulk transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: on shuttle and unit-train cycles the clock runs on the whole set, so loading and release windows are agreed before the train is ordered. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source covered hoppers on lease or per trip.

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Common questions

Can soybean meal be shipped by rail?

Yes. Soybean Meal moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Single cars and blocks to feed mills; unit trains to export.

How many tons of soybean meal fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 95–105 net tons per car.

What railcar does soybean meal move in?

Covered hopper (5,200+ cu ft). Car choice varies with grade and lane.

What is the bulk density of soybean meal?

Roughly 38–42 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle soybean meal rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate soybean meal carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in covered hoppers at 95–105 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
