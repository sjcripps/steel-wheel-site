Rail Freight Shipping in Jackson, MS

3 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Jackson is Mississippi's primary rail junction where Canadian National, Norfolk Southern, and CSX converge. CN's north-south corridor connects the Gulf Coast to Memphis, Chicago, and Canada, making Jackson a strategic interchange point. The city handles forest products, chemicals, and petroleum traffic serving the central Mississippi region.

Railroads Serving Jackson

Jackson is served by Canadian National, Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing extensive Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Jackson metro area.

Canadian National

Canadian National Railway operates a transcontinental network spanning Canada and the central U.S. from the Gulf Coast to the Great Lakes. CN handles petroleum, forest products, and grain.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.
