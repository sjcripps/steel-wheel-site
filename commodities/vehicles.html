Finished Vehicles Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

New cars and trucks move in enclosed autoracks from assembly plants and ports to distribution ramps — about 8–20 vehicles per rack depending on deck count and model size.

Finished vehicles logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates finished vehicles carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Finished vehicles load about 15–25 tons per rack and the vehicle count depends on deck level and model size, so we match bi-level or tri-level autoracks to the models and to the ramp on each end. Typical end uses are new vehicle distribution to dealer networks — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source autoracks on lease or per trip.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Kentucky: 6.8 million tons of transportation equipment moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2021 (Kentucky State Rail Plan 2025, p. 79).
  • Florida: 2.4 million tons of transportation equipment moved by rail in all directions in 2018 (Florida State Rail Plan 2023, p. 265).
  • Wyoming: 2.2 million tons of transportation equipment moved by rail in all directions - origin, destination, intrastate and through combined in 2011 (Wyoming State Rail Plan 2015, p. 120).
  • Georgia: 1.6 million tons of vehicles and transportation equipment moved by rail to, from and within the state in 2017 (Georgia State Rail Plan 2021, p. 15).
  • New Jersey: 1.5 million tons of transportation equipment moved by rail to, from, within and through the state in 2018 (New Jersey State Rail Plan 2025, p. 138).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Missouri: 2.3 million tons of motor vehicles moved by rail outbound from the state in 2018 (Missouri State Rail Plan 2022, p. 51).
  • Indiana: 1.8 million tons of transportation equipment originated by rail in the state in 2018 (Indiana State Rail Plan 2021, p. 125).
  • North Dakota: 1.5 million tons of transportation equipment moved through the state by rail as overhead traffic in 2019 (North Dakota State Rail Plan 2023, p. 139).
  • Colorado: 597,880 tons of motor vehicles moved by rail inbound to the state in 2019 (Colorado State Rail Plan 2024, p. 38).
  • South Carolina: 385,000 tons of transportation equipment moved by rail within the state in 2022 (South Carolina State Rail Plan 2024, p. 56).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can finished vehicles be shipped by rail?

Yes. Finished Vehicles moves regularly on the North American rail network in autoracks. Dedicated networks connect every assembly plant in North America; Mexico-built vehicles northbound are among the busiest cross-border flows..

How many tons of finished vehicles fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 15–25 net tons per car — the car fills up (cubes out) before it reaches its weight limit.

What railcar does finished vehicles move in?

Autorack (bi-level or tri-level). Because the commodity is light for its volume, shippers prefer the largest cubic capacity available.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle finished vehicles rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate finished vehicles carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in autoracks at 15–25 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
