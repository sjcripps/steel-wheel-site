Frequently asked questions

Where does railcar lease activity data come from?

From the Surface Transportation Board's Section of Recordations. Under 49 U.S.C. 11301, railcar leases, subleases, assignments and security agreements are recorded with the STB and the index is public. Each entry names the parties and the equipment, and links to the filed memorandum. This page aggregates that index every Monday.

Does the STB index show lease rates or terms?

No. A memorandum of lease names the lessor, the lessee and the cars, but rent and term are almost never stated. For current lease-rate ranges see our railcar lease rates tracker; for a renewal benchmark, use the Railcar Lease Renewal Desk.

Are all railcar leases recorded with the STB?

Most financed and large-lessor leases are, because recording perfects the lessor's or lender's interest. Short subleases and some direct deals are not. Treat the counts as a floor, and note that car counts are summed only where the filing states a number.
