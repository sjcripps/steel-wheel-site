Rail Logistics Services in Atlanta, GA

2 Class I Railroads · 3 Major Terminals

Atlanta is the Southeast's primary rail hub, served by both Norfolk Southern and CSX. Norfolk Southern's Inman Yard is one of the largest classification yards in the eastern U.S. The city serves as a critical interchange point for freight moving between the Port of Savannah and inland markets throughout the Southeast and Midwest.

Rail logistics services in Atlanta

A Atlanta shipper moving automotive, building materials and consumer goods by rail can route on Norfolk Southern or CSX, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between NS and CSX and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, building materials, consumer goods and paper products that means sourcing autoracks and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Atlanta (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Atlanta lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Atlanta

Atlanta is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Atlanta metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Common questions about rail freight in Atlanta

Which railroads serve Atlanta?

Atlanta, GA is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, with major facilities including Inman Yard and Tilford Yard. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle rail freight in Atlanta?

Yes. Steel Wheel Logistics is a national rail logistics company based in Petal, Mississippi, and works Atlanta lanes remotely as an outsourced rail department: routing, pricing, railroad coordination, transload, demurrage management and railcar sourcing. There is no walk-in location; the work is done by phone, email and the railroads' own systems.
