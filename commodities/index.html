Commodities by Rail

What each commodity is, which railcar it rides in, how many tons fit in a car, and where it flows across North America. Every entry links the interactive Commodity Flow Map, the Railcar Capacity Guide and the Rail Rate Quote tool.

Grain & Feed

  • Corn — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • Wheat — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Soybeans — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains) — Covered hopper, 95–105 tons/car
  • Soybean Meal — Covered hopper, 95–105 tons/car

Energy

  • Coal — Open-top hopper or rotary-dump gondola, 110–121 tons/car
  • Crude Oil — DOT-117 tank car, 90–100 tons/car
  • Ethanol — DOT-117 tank car, 85–95 tons/car
  • Propane / LPG — Pressure tank car, 60–70 tons/car
  • Petroleum Coke — Open-top hopper or rotary gondola, 100–115 tons/car

Minerals & Construction

  • Aggregates & Crushed Stone — Open-top hopper or gondola, 100–115 tons/car
  • Cement — Pressure-differential covered hopper, 100–115 tons/car
  • Frac Sand — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Iron Ore — Ore cars, 95–110 tons/car
  • Barite — Open-top or covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • Soda Ash — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Salt — Covered or open-top hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Potash — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car

Metals

  • Steel (Coils, Plate, Bar) — Coil car, gondola, bulkhead flat, 90–100 tons/car
  • Scrap Metal — Gondola, 80–100 tons/car

Forest Products

  • Lumber & Wood Products — Centerbeam flat, 75–95 tons/car
  • Paper & Pulp — High-cube boxcar, 70–90 tons/car
  • Wood Pellets — Covered hopper, 90–100 tons/car

Chemicals & Manufactured

  • Industrial Chemicals (Liquid) — General-service or specialized tank car, 80–100 tons/car
  • Dry Fertilizer (Urea, DAP, MAP) — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • Plastic Pellets (Resin) — Jumbo covered hopper, 85–95 tons/car
  • Synthetic Amorphous Silica — Pressure-differential covered hopper, 10–37 (grade-dependent) tons/car
  • Finished Vehicles — Autorack, 15–25 tons/car
