Common questions

Can propane / lpg be shipped by rail?

Yes. Propane / LPG moves regularly on the North American rail network in pressure tank cars. Strong fall/winter seasonality; Canadian LPG also rails to the West Coast for export..

How many tons of propane / lpg fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 60–70 net tons per car.

What railcar does propane / lpg move in?

Pressure tank car (DOT-112, ~33,500 gal). Car choice varies with grade and lane.
