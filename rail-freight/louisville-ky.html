Rail Freight Shipping in Louisville, KY

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Louisville is a major Ohio River rail hub served by CSX and Norfolk Southern. The city's Ford assembly plants and UPS Worldport distribution hub drive significant automotive and consumer goods rail traffic. Louisville also handles bourbon and spirits shipments from the surrounding Kentucky distillery region, along with coal from Appalachian mines.

Railroads Serving Louisville

Louisville is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Louisville metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.
