Crude Oil Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Crude by rail connects production basins to refineries the pipelines don't reach — primarily Bakken and Western Canadian barrels to coastal refineries.

Crude oil logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates crude oil carload moves nationwide — single cars, multi-car blocks and unit-train sets — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Crude oil is weight-limited at about 90–100 tons per car, so tank car size, lining and fittings are matched to the product and to the receiver's unloading connections before a car is ordered. Typical end uses are refinery feedstock — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a tank transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: on shuttle and unit-train cycles the clock runs on the whole set, so loading and release windows are agreed before the train is ordered. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source DOT-117 tank cars on lease or per trip.

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can crude oil be shipped by rail?

Yes. Crude Oil moves regularly on the North American rail network in dot-117 tank cars. Unit trains of DOT-117s; volume swings with the pipeline-vs-rail price spread.

How many tons of crude oil fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 90–100 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does crude oil move in?

DOT-117 tank car (~28,000–30,000 gal). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle crude oil rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate crude oil carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in DOT-117 tank cars at 90–100 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
