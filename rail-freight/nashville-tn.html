Rail Freight Shipping in Nashville, TN

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Nashville is a growing rail hub in the mid-South served by CSX and Norfolk Southern. CSX's Radnor Yard is a key classification facility for freight moving through the Tennessee Valley. The city's booming economy is driving increased freight volumes and distribution center development along rail corridors.

Railroads Serving Nashville

Nashville is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Nashville metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around Nashville include:
