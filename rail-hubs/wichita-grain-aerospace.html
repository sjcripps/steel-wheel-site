Wichita (Grain & Aerospace)

Origin region · Wichita, KS · Class I service: BNSF, UP

Hard red winter wheat country — major grain elevator concentration. Spirit AeroSystems and Textron (Cessna/Beechcraft) generate significant rail traffic for aerospace materials inbound. BNSF and UP both serve the city.

Commodities through this hub

  • Corn by rail — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • Wheat by rail — Covered hopper, 100–112 tons/car
  • Soybeans by rail — Covered hopper, 100–110 tons/car
  • DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains) by rail — Covered hopper, 95–105 tons/car
  • Soybean Meal by rail — Covered hopper, 95–105 tons/car
  • Ethanol by rail — DOT-117 tank car, 85–95 tons/car
