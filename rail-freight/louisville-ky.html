Rail Logistics Services in Louisville, KY

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Louisville is a major Ohio River rail hub served by CSX and Norfolk Southern. The city's Ford assembly plants and UPS Worldport distribution hub drive significant automotive and consumer goods rail traffic. Louisville also handles bourbon and spirits shipments from the surrounding Kentucky distillery region, along with coal from Appalachian mines.

Rail logistics services in Louisville

A Louisville shipper moving automotive, coal and bourbon/spirits by rail can route on CSX or Norfolk Southern, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between CSX and NS and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For automotive, coal, bourbon/spirits and consumer goods that means sourcing autoracks, open-top hoppers and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Louisville's port access adds a rail-to-vessel option for export and import cargo; we arrange the transload leg and the terminal handoff so the rail move and the vessel schedule line up. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Louisville lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.

Railroads Serving Louisville

Louisville is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Louisville metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Common questions about rail freight in Louisville

Which railroads serve Louisville?

Louisville, KY is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, with major facilities including Osborn Yard and Ford Assembly rail facility. Short lines and switching carriers connect individual plants and transload sites to those Class I networks.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle rail freight in Louisville?

Yes. Steel Wheel Logistics is a national rail logistics company based in Petal, Mississippi, and works Louisville lanes remotely as an outsourced rail department: routing, pricing, railroad coordination, transload, demurrage management and railcar sourcing. There is no walk-in location; the work is done by phone, email and the railroads' own systems.
