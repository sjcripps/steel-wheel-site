Rail Freight Shipping in Billings, MT

1 Class I Railroad · 2 Major Terminals

Billings is the largest city in Montana and a key point on BNSF's northern transcontinental corridor. The nearby Laurel Yard is BNSF's main classification facility in the region. Coal from the Powder River Basin, petroleum products from Montana and North Dakota refineries, and grain from the northern Great Plains dominate rail traffic through Billings.

Railroads Serving Billings

Billings is served by BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Billings metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for intermodal, coal, grain, and industrial products.

Major rail facilities in and around Billings include:
