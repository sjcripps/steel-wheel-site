Rail Freight Shipping in Hattiesburg, MS

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Hattiesburg is a south Mississippi rail junction where Canadian National and Norfolk Southern lines intersect. The city serves the region's timber and paper industries, with forest products representing a major share of rail volume. CN's mainline through Hattiesburg connects the Gulf Coast ports to Jackson and points north.

Railroads Serving Hattiesburg

Hattiesburg is served by Canadian National and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Hattiesburg metro area.

Canadian National

Canadian National Railway operates a transcontinental network spanning Canada and the central U.S. from the Gulf Coast to the Great Lakes. CN handles intermodal, petroleum, forest products, and grain.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for intermodal, coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.
