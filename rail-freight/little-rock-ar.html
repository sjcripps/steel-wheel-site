Rail Freight Shipping in Little Rock, AR

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Little Rock is central Arkansas's rail hub, served by Union Pacific and BNSF. The city sits on the Arkansas River where rail and barge freight interchange. Agricultural products, lumber, and steel move through Little Rock's rail terminals, connecting the mid-South region to national rail networks.

Railroads Serving Little Rock

Little Rock is served by Union Pacific and BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Little Rock metro area.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.
