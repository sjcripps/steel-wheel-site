Port of Port Arthur, TX rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are mineral fuels (about $3,838 million), wood pulp (about $131 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CPKC, Union Pacific and BNSF Railway, which is what makes the Port Arthur gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland.
