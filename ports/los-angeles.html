Port of Los Angeles, CA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are plastics and resin (about $5,660 million), mineral fuels (about $4,464 million), rubber (about $2,925 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks.
