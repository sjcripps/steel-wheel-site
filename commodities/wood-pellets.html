Wood Pellets Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Densified sawmill residuals, mostly bound for European power plants — the US Southeast is the world's largest exporter.

Wood pellets logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates wood pellets carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. At 40–45 lb per cubic foot wood pellets load close to both limits at once (90–100 tons per car), so we match covered hoppers to the receiver's unloading method and check weight and cube on every load plan. Typical end uses are utility-scale renewable generation (export), home heating pellets (domestic) — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a bulk transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source covered hoppers on lease or per trip.

Common questions

Can wood pellets be shipped by rail?

Yes. Wood Pellets moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Southeast pellet plants rail to dedicated export terminals at Savannah, Mobile and the Mississippi River; moisture control matters, so covered equipment throughout..

How many tons of wood pellets fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 90–100 net tons per car.

What railcar does wood pellets move in?

Covered hopper. Car choice varies with grade and lane.

What is the bulk density of wood pellets?

Roughly 40–45 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle wood pellets rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate wood pellets carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in covered hoppers at 90–100 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
