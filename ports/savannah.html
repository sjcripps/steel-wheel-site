Port of Savannah, GA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are plastics and resin (about $3,027 million), rubber (about $1,711 million), steel articles and pipe (about $1,247 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CSX and Norfolk Southern, which is what makes the Savannah gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
