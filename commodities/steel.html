Steel Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Finished and semi-finished steel — coils ride in purpose-built covered coil cars, plate and structural in gondolas and flats.

Steel logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates steel carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Steel weighs out at 90–100 tons per car well before the car is full, so we size coil cars, gondolas or bulkhead flats to the weight limit and the receiving track's capacity rather than to cube. Typical end uses are automotive, construction, appliances, pipe and tube, service centers — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a team track or rail-served warehouse near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source coil cars, gondolas or bulkhead flats on lease or per trip.

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Yes. Steel (Coils, Plate, Bar) moves regularly on the North American rail network in coil car, gondola, bulkhead flats. Mill-to-service-center and mill-to-stamper lanes around the Great Lakes, the Southeast's new EAF capacity, and cross-border Mexico flows..

How many tons of steel (coils, plate, bar) fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 90–100 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does steel (coils, plate, bar) move in?

Coil car, gondola, bulkhead flat. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.
