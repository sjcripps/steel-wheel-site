Rail Logistics Services in Gulfport, MS

1 Class I Railroad · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Gulfport is a Mississippi Gulf Coast port city served by CSX. The Port of Gulfport is the state's only deep-water seaport and handles forest products, frozen poultry, and breakbulk cargo with direct rail access. CSX's Gulf Coast mainline connects Gulfport to New Orleans and Jacksonville.

Rail logistics services in Gulfport

A Gulfport shipper moving forest products, petroleum products and consumer goods by rail works with a single Class I, CSX, so the routing question is which interchange and which short line or switching carrier get the car to the far end at the best rate. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing on CSX and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For forest products, petroleum products, consumer goods and poultry products that means sourcing open-top hoppers, tank cars and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Gulfport's port access adds a rail-to-vessel option for export and import cargo; we arrange the transload leg and the terminal handoff so the rail move and the vessel schedule line up. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Gulfport lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Rate estimates are indicative until the serving railroad confirms them.
