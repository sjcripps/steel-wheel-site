Urea, DAP and MAP move from Gulf import terminals and domestic plants to inland distribution warehouses ahead of planting seasons.

Fertilizer logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates fertilizer carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Fertilizer weighs out at 100–110 tons per car (roughly 45–62 lb per cubic foot) well before the car is full, so we size covered hoppers to the weight limit and the receiving track's capacity rather than to cube. Typical end uses are row-crop nutrition — nitrogen (urea) and phosphate (DAP/MAP) applications — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a bulk transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source covered hoppers on lease or per trip, and on fertilizer we line up car supply ahead of the seasonal peak.

Common questions

Can dry fertilizer (urea, dap, map) be shipped by rail?

Yes. Dry Fertilizer (Urea, DAP, MAP) moves regularly on the North American rail network in covered hoppers. Strongly seasonal: heavy flows Jan–Apr and a fall run.

How many tons of dry fertilizer (urea, dap, map) fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 100–110 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does dry fertilizer (urea, dap, map) move in?

Covered hopper (3,000–4,750 cu ft). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of dry fertilizer (urea, dap, map)?

Roughly 45–62 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.

Yes — we price and coordinate fertilizer carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in covered hoppers at 100–110 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
