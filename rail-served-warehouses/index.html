Rail-Served Warehouse Directory

97 third-party warehouses across 31 states that take other companies' freight and sit on rail. Public warehousing, contract warehousing, bulk commodity storage and 3PL space — the kind you can rent, as opposed to a manufacturer's own plant or a retailer's distribution centre.

If your product has to go indoors — pulp, paper, food-grade, anything that cannot sit in a yard — a rail-served warehouse lets the railcar come to the building. Most transload sites are outdoor bulk transfer, which is a different service; those are in the transload directory.
