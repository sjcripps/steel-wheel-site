Rail Freight Shipping in Meridian, MS

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Meridian is a historic rail junction in east Mississippi where Norfolk Southern and Canadian National share the Meridian Speedway — a high-capacity corridor connecting Meridian to Shreveport. The Speedway allows NS trains to bypass congested routes, making Meridian a strategic point for intermodal and merchandise freight moving between the Southeast and Texas.

Railroads Serving Meridian

Meridian is served by Norfolk Southern and Canadian National, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Meridian metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for intermodal, coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Canadian National

Canadian National Railway operates a transcontinental network spanning Canada and the central U.S. from the Gulf Coast to the Great Lakes. CN handles intermodal, petroleum, forest products, and grain.

Major rail facilities in and around Meridian include:
