Demurrage Inputs

Debit Days = total calendar days the railroad held the car, minus non-chargeable holidays (manifest trains only). The calculator subtracts free credit days automatically. For binding numbers, Class I railroads compute Debit Days from constructive placement timestamps.

Tip: Free credit days are per car, not per shipment. 5 cars held 3 days each = 5 separate 2-day credit windows, not "15 days collectively." This calculator handles that automatically.
