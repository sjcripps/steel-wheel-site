Paper Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Rolls of containerboard, printing paper and market pulp move in clean, cushioned high-cube boxcars from Southern and Canadian mills.

Paper logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates paper carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Paper fills the car before it reaches the weight limit, topping out near 70–90 tons, so we spec the largest-cube high-cube boxcars the lane allows and check the load plan against the receiver's unloading setup. Typical end uses are corrugated boxes, tissue, printing, export pulp — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a team track or rail-served warehouse near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source high-cube boxcars on lease or per trip.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Kentucky: 6.7 million tons of pulp, paper or allied products moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2021 (Kentucky State Rail Plan 2025, p. 79).
  • Georgia: 5.5 million tons of pulp, paper and allied products moved by rail to, from and within the state (excluding pass-through traffic) in 2017 (Georgia State Rail Plan 2021, p. 97).
  • Florida: 3.9 million tons of pulp, paper or allied products moved by rail in all directions in 2018 (Florida State Rail Plan 2023, p. 265).
  • Mississippi: 2.2 million tons of pulp, paper or allied products moved by rail to, from and within the state (excluding through traffic) in 2017 (Mississippi State Rail Plan 2022, p. 112).
  • Idaho: 2.2 million tons of pulp, newsprint, paper and paperboard moved by rail into, out of, within or through the state (through traffic included) in 2010 (Idaho State Rail Plan 2013, p. 65).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Louisiana: 2.5 million tons of fiber, paper, or pulpboard moved by rail outbound from the state in 2021 (Louisiana State Rail Plan 2024, p. 61).
  • Oklahoma: 1.3 million tons of pulp, paper or allied products moved by rail outbound from the state in 2019 (Oklahoma State Rail Plan 2021, p. 101).
  • South Carolina: 1.2 million tons of pulp, paper, or allied products moved by rail outbound from the state in 2022 (South Carolina State Rail Plan 2024, p. 55).
  • Wisconsin: 988,748 tons of pulp or pulp mill products terminated by rail in the state in 2019 (Wisconsin State Rail Plan 2026, p. 139).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can paper & pulp be shipped by rail?

Yes. Paper & Pulp moves regularly on the North American rail network in high-cube boxcars. Mill-to-converter lanes across the Southeast and cross-border from Eastern Canada; the boxcar fleet serving this traffic is aging, which keeps car supply tight..

How many tons of paper & pulp fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 70–90 net tons per car — the car fills up (cubes out) before it reaches its weight limit.

What railcar does paper & pulp move in?

High-cube boxcar (60 ft, cushioned). Because the commodity is light for its volume, shippers prefer the largest cubic capacity available.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle paper rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate paper carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in high-cube boxcars at 70–90 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
