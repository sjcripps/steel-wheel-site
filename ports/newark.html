Port of Newark, NJ rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are mineral fuels (about $8,137 million), plastics and resin (about $3,779 million), organic chemicals (about $2,498 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CSX and Norfolk Southern, which is what makes the Newark gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. Port Newark-Elizabeth; Conrail Shared Assets.
