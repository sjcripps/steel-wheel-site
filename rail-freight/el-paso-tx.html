Rail Freight Shipping in El Paso, TX

2 Class I Railroads · 3 Major Terminals

El Paso is one of the busiest U.S.-Mexico rail border crossings, with BNSF and Union Pacific operating international gateways that connect to Mexican rail networks. Cross-border trade drives massive merchandise and automotive volumes. The city is a critical link in the supply chain connecting Mexican manufacturing with U.S. markets.

Railroads Serving El Paso

El Paso is served by BNSF and Union Pacific, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the El Paso metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around El Paso include:

  • El Paso Yard (BNSF)
  • Alfalfa Yard (UP)
  • International bridges to Ciudad Juarez

These facilities handle car classification, transload, and bulk commodity loading and unloading operations. Learn how classification yards sort and route freight in our article: How Railroad Classification Yards Work.

Multimodal Connections

El Paso's rail infrastructure connects to the broader national network, providing access to ports, distribution centers, and production facilities across the country. Multiple Class I railroad connections give shippers routing flexibility and competitive rate options.
