Rail Freight Shipping in Charleston, SC

2 Class I Railroads · 1 Major Terminal ·Port Access

Charleston is a growing Southeast port city where CSX and Norfolk Southern connect international container trade to inland markets. The South Carolina Ports Authority has invested heavily in rail infrastructure, including the Inland Port at Dillon, which extends the port's reach into the Carolinas and Virginia via Norfolk Southern rail service.

Railroads Serving Charleston

Charleston is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Charleston metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Multimodal & Port Connections

Port of Charleston — deep-water container port with dedicated rail-served inland port at Dillon, SC.

Port access gives Charleston shippers the advantage of rail-to-vessel transloading for international trade. Whether you are exporting or importing bulk commodities, the combination of rail and port infrastructure creates efficient multimodal supply chain options.
