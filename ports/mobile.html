Port of Mobile, AL rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are mineral fuels (about $1,810 million), iron and steel (about $1,759 million), steel articles and pipe (about $252 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CSX, Norfolk Southern, CN, CPKC and BNSF Railway, which is what makes the Mobile gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland. Five Class Is; Pinto Island slabs, Alabama Steel Terminals coil. This page pairs the Census import data behind the Commodity Flow Map port layer with the railroads, transload capacity and inland lanes that turn a vessel arrival into a rail move.
