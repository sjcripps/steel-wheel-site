Coal by Rail

Still one of the largest single commodities on US rails. Powder River Basin thermal coal and Appalachian met coal move in dedicated unit trains from mine to plant or port.

RailcarOpen-top hopper or rotary-dump gondola
Typical net load110–121 tons per car
Bulk density~45–55 lb/cu ft
Loading constraintWeighs out (weight-limited)
STCC11212 — look up related codes in the STCC Finder

What it's used for

Electric generation (thermal), steelmaking coke (metallurgical), export.

How it moves

100–150 car unit trains in captive cycles; rotary couplers let gondolas dump without uncoupling. Met coal rails east to Baltimore/Hampton Roads for export.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Wyoming: 479.2 million tons of coal moved by rail in all directions - origin, destination, intrastate and through combined in 2011 (Wyoming State Rail Plan 2015, p. 120).
  • Missouri: 135.5 million tons of bituminous coal moved by rail to, from, within and through the state in 2018 (Missouri State Rail Plan 2022, p. 50).
  • Illinois: 123.1 million tons of coal handled by the state's rail network in all directions (including through traffic) in 2019 (Illinois State Rail Plan 2023, p. 267).
  • Tennessee: 91.6 million tons of coal moved by rail in all directions in 2012 (Tennessee State Rail Plan 2019, p. 80).
  • Iowa: 64.7 million tons of coal moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2022 (Iowa State Rail Plan 2025, p. 246).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Kansas: 174 million tons of coal moved by rail through the state in 2007 (Kansas State Rail Plan 2011, p. 96).
  • South Dakota: 78.6 million tons of coal moved through the state by rail in 2019 (South Dakota State Rail Plan 2022, p. 112).
  • Oklahoma: 58 million tons of coal moved through the state by rail in 2019 (Oklahoma State Rail Plan 2021, p. 109).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can coal be shipped by rail?

Yes. Coal moves regularly on the North American rail network in open-top hopper or rotary-dump gondolas. 100–150 car unit trains in captive cycles; rotary couplers let gondolas dump without uncoupling.

How many tons of coal fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 110–121 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does coal move in?

Open-top hopper or rotary-dump gondola. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

What is the bulk density of coal?

Roughly 45–55 lb per cubic foot, depending on grade and condition.
