What this is

Your saved-lane watchlist. Add the lanes you ship — origin, destination, commodity, car type — and we track the indicative rate, the planning-model transit time, and how your railroads are running, refreshed weekly. When something moves on a lane you care about, you'll see it here instead of finding out on an invoice.

The two tools that do the work live next door:

Rate Estimator → Run an indicative rate + transit estimate on any lane. Save the ones worth watching to this dashboard. BoL Builder → Generate a compliant rail bill of lading (PDF/Word) for a saved lane — domestic or cross-border.
