Rail Freight Shipping in Los Angeles, CA

2 Class I Railroads · 4 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Los Angeles is the western gateway for international trade, with the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach generating massive intermodal rail volumes. BNSF and Union Pacific operate major intermodal yards that funnel containerized freight from Asia-Pacific trade routes onto transcontinental rail corridors serving the entire U.S. market.

Railroads Serving Los Angeles

Los Angeles is served by BNSF and Union Pacific, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Los Angeles metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for intermodal, coal, grain, and industrial products.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including intermodal, industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around Los Angeles include:

  • Hobart Yard (BNSF)
  • ICTF (UP)
  • East Los Angeles Yard (UP)
  • San Bernardino Intermodal (BNSF)

These facilities handle car classification, intermodal transfers, and bulk commodity loading and unloading operations. Learn how classification yards sort and route freight in our article: How Railroad Classification Yards Work.

Commodities Shipped by Rail

Key commodities moving by rail through Los Angeles include:

  • Intermodal containers
  • Consumer goods

Intermodal & Port Connections

Port of Los Angeles and Port of Long Beach — the nation's busiest container port complex with dedicated on-dock rail facilities.

Port access gives Los Angeles shippers the advantage of rail-to-vessel transloading for international trade. Whether you are exporting bulk commodities or importing containerized freight, the combination of rail and port infrastructure creates efficient multimodal supply chain options.
