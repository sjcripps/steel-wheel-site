Appalachian Coal Country (Met & Thermal)

Origin region · Appalachian Coal Country, WV · Class I service: CSX, NS

Metallurgical coal for steelmaking and thermal coal. CSX and NS built their networks around this corridor. Moves to Virginia/Baltimore ports for export or west to Midwest steel mills.

Commodities through this hub

  • Coal by rail — Open-top hopper or rotary-dump gondola, 110–121 tons/car
  • Petroleum Coke by rail — Open-top hopper or rotary gondola, 100–115 tons/car
