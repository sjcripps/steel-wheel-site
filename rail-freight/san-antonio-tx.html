Rail Freight Shipping in San Antonio, TX

2 Class I Railroads · 3 Major Terminals

San Antonio sits on major Union Pacific and BNSF corridors connecting the Texas-Mexico border region with the interior U.S. The city handles significant building materials and petroleum products traffic, and its proximity to the Eagle Ford Shale region drives energy-related rail shipments.

Railroads Serving San Antonio

San Antonio is served by Union Pacific and BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the San Antonio metro area.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including intermodal, industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for intermodal, coal, grain, and industrial products.
