Rail Freight Shipping in Jacksonville, FL

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals ·Port Access

Jacksonville is CSX's hometown and the rail gateway to Florida. The city's terminals handle merchandise freight destined for the Florida peninsula, along with phosphate moving from central Florida mines. JAXPORT's expanding container operations are adding international trade volumes to the city's rail network.

Railroads Serving Jacksonville

Jacksonville is served by CSX and Norfolk Southern, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Jacksonville metro area.

CSX

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Key Rail Terminals & Yards

Major rail facilities in and around Jacksonville include:

  • Moncrief Yard (CSX)
  • Simpson Yard (CSX)

Multimodal & Port Connections

JAXPORT — growing container port with on-dock rail capabilities.
