Outsourced Rail Department

Steel Wheel Logistics is a rail logistics company. We run the rail function for shippers who move bulk commodities by rail and do not have, or do not want to build, a rail desk of their own. The services below can be engaged a piece at a time. Most shippers without a rail person on staff want all of it, ongoing — that’s our outsourced rail department: routing and pricing, carrier outreach, car supply, demurrage, transload, and railcar brokerage, handled week over week so you don’t have to build the expertise in-house.

We route and price your rail moves and deal with the railroads on your behalf. Working directly with Class I carriers (BNSF, UP, CSX, NS, CN, CPKC) and regional short lines is slow and full of jargon; we have the contacts and the patience to reach the right people and get real rates back, so you can stop chasing. Run an indicative rail freight quote first if you want a ballpark before we source the carrier number.

  • Carrier outreach and rate sourcing across Class Is and short lines
  • Help structuring origin/destination pairs to find favorable lanes
  • Short line and switching carrier coordination
  • Car supply coordination — getting equipment when and where you need it

Demurrage Control

Demurrage is the number one surprise cost for rail shippers, and most of it is preventable. We find the operational causes behind the charges, pre-plan car supply and loading windows to keep your free time intact, and open the right conversation with the railroad on the charges that should not stand.

  • Review demurrage charges to identify root causes (loading/unloading delays, car flow, weekend timing)
  • Help open the right conversation with the railroad on questionable charges
  • Storage-in-transit and free-time guidance
  • Demurrage contract review — flagging terms worth a second look

Try our free demurrage calculator to estimate charges on a specific scenario.

Equipment Coordination

Whether you run your own railcars or pull from the railroad’s pool, the day-to-day tracking, ordering, and diverting still falls on the shipper. We handle that coordination so you can focus on the load.

  • Real-time car tracking and trip monitoring (private fleets)
  • Shipper’s pool monitoring and equipment ordering (railroad-controlled)
  • Empty and loaded diversion coordination
  • Connect you with vetted repair, cleaning, and storage vendors
  • “Best route” guidance for empties and reloads

Railcar Brokerage

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of company is Steel Wheel Logistics?

A rail logistics company. General logistics providers cover every mode — truck, rail, ocean, air — and treat rail as a side line. We don’t. We’re rail only: an outsourced rail department for shippers, and a railcar broker on the equipment side. Working directly with the railroads is slow and full of jargon, so our job is to make it easier for you — sourcing rates, coordinating carriers, and solving rail problems day in and day out. We coordinate the move; we don’t haul the freight. If your shipment doesn’t belong on rail, we’ll tell you.

What’s the minimum volume you’ll work with?

We work with small and mid-sized shippers. Single carloads and manifest shipments are our bread and butter — if you’re moving at least one car a month on a steady lane, we can help. Larger volumes scale up the same way. Read our first-time rail shipper guide if you’re just starting.

Which railroads do you work with?

All Class I carriers — BNSF, Union Pacific, CSX, Norfolk Southern, CN, CPKC — plus regional short lines and terminal carriers where those connections matter. Each has different pricing dynamics and different responsiveness. We help you pick the right routing and reach the right people.

What about demurrage — can you help us avoid it?

Yes. Demurrage is the #1 surprise cost for new rail shippers. We pre-plan car supply, track constructive placement, and coordinate loading windows to keep your free time intact. Our demurrage avoidance guide covers the basics.

Do you handle transloading?

Yes. We coordinate rail-to-truck handoffs at transload facilities across the network. If your customer isn’t rail-served, we’ll design a route that gets the commodity there via rail for most of the distance and truck for the last leg.

Can you help us find or place railcars?

Yes. On the equipment side we work as a railcar broker: sourcing lease and sublease cars when you are short, placing surplus cars when you are long, and reviewing lease renewals before you sign. See our railcar brokerage page.

How does pricing work?

Rail freight pricing has several layers — line-haul rates, fuel surcharges, accessorials, and car hire. We help you source rates across the full stack, not just the base line-haul. See how rail pricing actually works for a breakdown, or run a quick estimate with our rail rate calculator.
