Rail Logistics Services in San Antonio, TX

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

San Antonio sits on major Union Pacific and BNSF corridors connecting the Texas-Mexico border region with the interior U.S. The city handles significant building materials and petroleum products traffic, and its proximity to the Eagle Ford Shale region drives energy-related rail shipments.

Rail logistics services in San Antonio

A San Antonio shipper moving building materials, petroleum products and consumer goods by rail can route on Union Pacific or BNSF, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between UP and BNSF and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For building materials, petroleum products, consumer goods and military freight that means sourcing tank cars, boxcars and open-top hoppers, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near San Antonio (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work San Antonio lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving San Antonio

San Antonio is served by Union Pacific and BNSF, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the San Antonio metro area.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.
