Port of Philadelphia, PA rail freight: what lands here and where it goes inland

The largest vessel imports here, year to date through July 2026, are meat (about $4,470 million), mineral fuels (about $2,233 million), fruit and nuts (about $1,932 million) among the 22 commodity chapters Steel Wheel Logistics tracks. The port district is reached by CSX and Norfolk Southern, which is what makes the Philadelphia gateway useful for shippers whose plants sit hundreds of miles inland.
