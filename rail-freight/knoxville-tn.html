Rail Logistics Services in Knoxville, TN

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Knoxville is an east Tennessee rail hub served by Norfolk Southern and CSX. The city sits at the junction of rail corridors passing through the Appalachian coal fields, handling significant coal traffic from eastern Kentucky and Virginia mines. NS's mainline through Knoxville connects the Southeast to the Ohio Valley.

Rail logistics services in Knoxville

A Knoxville shipper moving coal, grain and automotive by rail can route on Norfolk Southern or CSX, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between NS and CSX and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For coal, grain, automotive and chemicals that means sourcing open-top hoppers, covered hoppers and autoracks, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Knoxville (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Knoxville lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Knoxville is served by Norfolk Southern and CSX, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Knoxville metro area.

CSX Transportation operates a rail network across 23 eastern states, connecting major ports, production centers, and population hubs. CSX handles coal, chemicals, automotive, and agricultural freight.
