Rail Logistics Services in Meridian, MS

2 Class I Railroads · 2 Major Terminals

Meridian is a historic rail junction in east Mississippi where Norfolk Southern and Canadian National share the Meridian Speedway — a high-capacity corridor connecting Meridian to Shreveport. The Speedway allows NS trains to bypass congested routes, making Meridian a strategic point for merchandise freight moving between the Southeast and Texas.

Rail logistics services in Meridian

A Meridian shipper moving lumber, chemicals and paper products by rail can route on Norfolk Southern or Canadian National, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between NS and CN and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For lumber, chemicals, paper products and aggregates that means sourcing centerbeam flatcars, tank cars and boxcars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Meridian (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Meridian lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Meridian

Meridian is served by Norfolk Southern and Canadian National, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Meridian metro area.

Norfolk Southern

Norfolk Southern Railway operates a major rail network in the eastern United States, serving 22 states. NS is a leading carrier for coal, automotive, and merchandise freight.

Canadian National

Canadian National Railway operates a transcontinental network spanning Canada and the central U.S. from the Gulf Coast to the Great Lakes. CN handles petroleum, forest products, and grain.
