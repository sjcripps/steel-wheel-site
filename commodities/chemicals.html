Chemicals logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates chemicals carload moves nationwide — single cars and multi-car blocks — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Chemicals are weight-limited at about 80–100 tons per car, so tank car size, lining and fittings are matched to the product and to the receiver's unloading connections before a car is ordered. Typical end uses are feedstocks and process chemicals for nearly every manufacturing industry — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a tank transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: manifest cars arrive bunched, so we set release rules with the receiver and watch free time car by car. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source general-service or specialized tank cars on lease or per trip.

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

Common questions

Can industrial chemicals (liquid) be shipped by rail?

Yes. Industrial Chemicals (Liquid) moves regularly on the North American rail network in general-service or specialized tank cars. Mostly single cars and small blocks in manifest service; car type, lining and fittings are matched to each product.

How many tons of industrial chemicals (liquid) fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 80–100 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does industrial chemicals (liquid) move in?

General-service or specialized tank car. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle chemicals rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate chemicals carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in general-service or specialized tank cars at 80–100 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
