What it's used for

Feedstocks and process chemicals for nearly every manufacturing industry.

How it moves

Mostly single cars and small blocks in manifest service; car type, lining and fittings are matched to each product. Hazmat rules govern routing and handling.

By the numbers: state rail plan figures

How much of this commodity group moves by rail, as published in the states' own federally mandated rail plans:

Statewide rail tonnage

  • Illinois: 104.2 million tons of chemicals and allied products handled by the state's rail network in all directions (including through traffic) in 2019 (Illinois State Rail Plan 2023, p. 267).
  • Texas: 82.9 million tons of chemicals or allied products moved by rail in all directions in 2022 (Texas State Rail Plan 2024, p. 169).
  • Iowa: 41.2 million tons of chemicals or allied products moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2022 (Iowa State Rail Plan 2025, p. 246).
  • Kentucky: 36.6 million tons of chemicals or allied products moved by rail in all directions across the state (including through traffic) in 2021 (Kentucky State Rail Plan 2025, p. 79).
  • Wyoming: 20.4 million tons of chemicals or allied products moved by rail in all directions - origin, destination, intrastate and through combined in 2011 (Wyoming State Rail Plan 2015, p. 120).

Single-direction figures

These count movement in one direction only — originated, inbound or outbound as each plan defines it — so they are not comparable with the statewide totals above.

  • Missouri: 16.6 million tons of miscellaneous industrial organic chemicals moved through the state by rail in 2018 (Missouri State Rail Plan 2022, p. 52).
  • Utah: 11.6 million tons of basic chemicals originated by rail in the state in 2011 (Utah State Rail Plan 2015, p. 121).

Each figure is quoted from the cited state rail plan and reflects that plan's own data year, commodity grouping and methodology — figures from different plans are not directly comparable with each other. Some plans count traffic passing through the state and others exclude it; each line says which.

Common questions

Can industrial chemicals (liquid) be shipped by rail?

Yes. Industrial Chemicals (Liquid) moves regularly on the North American rail network in general-service or specialized tank cars. Mostly single cars and small blocks in manifest service; car type, lining and fittings are matched to each product.

How many tons of industrial chemicals (liquid) fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 80–100 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does industrial chemicals (liquid) move in?

General-service or specialized tank car. Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.
