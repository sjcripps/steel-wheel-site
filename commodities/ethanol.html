Ethanol Rail Shipping & Logistics: Rates, Railcars, Lanes

Fuel ethanol cannot move in product pipelines (it absorbs water), so nearly all of it rails from Corn Belt plants to blending terminals.

Ethanol logistics by rail: what we do

Steel Wheel Logistics prices and coordinates ethanol carload moves nationwide — single cars, multi-car blocks and unit-train sets — from routing and an indicative rate per car through car ordering, release and tracing to placement. Ethanol is weight-limited at about 85–95 tons per car, so tank car size, lining and fittings are matched to the product and to the receiver's unloading connections before a car is ordered. Typical end uses are gasoline blending (E10/E15/E85), industrial alcohol — we confirm the receiving track and unloading method before the first car is ordered. Where the plant has no spur, we set up transload at a tank transload terminal near the receiver and price the rail-plus-truck move against straight truck, so the mode decision is made on numbers. Demurrage control is planned rather than reactive: on shuttle and unit-train cycles the clock runs on the whole set, so loading and release windows are agreed before the train is ordered. Shippers who need equipment can use our railcar brokerage to source DOT-117 tank cars on lease or per trip.

Common questions

Can ethanol be shipped by rail?

Yes. Ethanol moves regularly on the North American rail network in dot-117 tank cars. Unit trains from Iowa/Nebraska/Illinois plants to coastal and metro blending racks — one of the steadiest tank-car franchises on the network..

How many tons of ethanol fit in a railcar?

A typical load is 85–95 net tons per car — the car reaches its weight limit well before its cubic capacity.

What railcar does ethanol move in?

DOT-117 tank car (~28,500–30,000 gal). Because the commodity is dense, smaller-cube cars are common — the weight limit governs.

Does Steel Wheel Logistics handle ethanol rail logistics?

Yes — we price and coordinate ethanol carload moves nationwide: routing and indicative rates per car in DOT-117 tank cars at 85–95 tons, car supply through our railcar brokerage, transload where the plant has no spur, and demurrage control.
