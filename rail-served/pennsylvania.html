Rail-Served Businesses in Pennsylvania

352 rail-served businesses and industrial sites in Pennsylvania, grouped by serving railroad. Every listing here is high-confidence: either spur-verified from public map data (OpenStreetMap spur geometry cross-referenced with FRA rail-network ownership) or listed as a rail shipper in the Pennsylvania State Rail Plan — each entry carries its evidence. A rail-served neighbor is the strongest signal that rail economics can work at a location near you.

Moving freight to or from Pennsylvania? Transload facilities in Pennsylvania move bulk freight between railcar and truck when a site has no siding of its own, and the Rail Rate Quote tool gives an indicative estimate for your lane.
