Rail Logistics Services in Fort Worth, TX

2 Class I Railroads · 1 Major Terminal

Fort Worth is BNSF's headquarters city and home to the Alliance logistics hub, one of the largest inland ports in the country. The Tower 55 junction, where BNSF and UP lines cross, is one of the most critical rail bottlenecks in North America. Major investments are underway to improve rail fluidity through this corridor.

Rail logistics services in Fort Worth

A Fort Worth shipper moving grain, consumer goods and building materials by rail can route on BNSF or Union Pacific, and the two carriers rarely price the same lane the same way. Steel Wheel Logistics is that shipper's outsourced rail department: we price the lane, choose the routing between BNSF and UP and handle the railroad coordination that most plants no longer staff in-house. For grain, consumer goods, building materials and petroleum products that means sourcing covered hoppers, boxcars and tank cars, managing car ordering and supply, and watching demurrage before it accrues. Plants without their own rail spur can still ship by rail through a transload site near Fort Worth (see the facilities below), riding rail for the long haul and truck for the last few miles. We are a national company based in Petal, Mississippi, and work Fort Worth lanes remotely, by phone and email, the same way shippers work with the railroads themselves. Railroads Serving Fort Worth

Fort Worth is served by BNSF and Union Pacific, providing solid Class I railroad coverage for freight shippers in the Fort Worth metro area.

BNSF

BNSF Railway operates one of the largest rail networks in North America, covering the western two-thirds of the United States. BNSF is a major carrier for coal, grain, and industrial products.

Union Pacific

Union Pacific Railroad operates the largest rail network in the U.S., spanning 23 states across the western two-thirds of the country. UP handles diverse freight including industrial, coal, and agricultural products.
